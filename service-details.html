What the audience sees

A spectator freely selects a small packet. Four kings are counted cleanly. Then, one by one, each king vanishes without cover. The final king disappears while the spectator holds the cards—leaving a moment of silence before the applause.

Skill requirements

Conjurer-level pieces are not “hard.” They’re precise. This routine rewards rehearsal and calm hands.

Handling

Angles
